Adapa Dialer – Privacy Policy

Effective 10 October 2026

Adapa Dialer is the phone app used by Adapa Group's call-center staff. It is distributed only to Adapa staff and is not offered to the public. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices available. "We" and "Adapa" mean Adapa Group.

Who can use the app

Only Adapa employees and contractors with an Adapa staff account. Signing in needs a mobile number registered by Adapa and a one-time password (OTP). The app is not meant for children.

Calls through the call center are recorded

Calls placed through the call center (and calls customers make to Adapa's call-center numbers) are connected by our call-center service provider, which records the conversation and keeps call details such as the numbers, time and duration. Recordings are used for quality, training and resolving customer queries. Calls made or received directly on a SIM, without the call center, are not recorded by Adapa; when a customer calls a staff member's company SIM directly, the app labels the call "not recorded".

Information that stays on your phone

To work as your phone app, Adapa Dialer reads the following on the phone. The app does not send any of it to Adapa or anyone else:

  • Contacts: to show names and photos, search, and favourites. If you choose to add or star a contact, the app saves that change in your phone's contacts.
  • Call history: to show Recents and call details.
  • Phone and SIM information: to place and answer calls and to tell your company SIM from a personal SIM.
  • App records: a list of calls you placed through the call center, call-center numbers the app has learned, your choices such as "Always use this choice for…", and your settings. Your call-center call list is cleared when you sign out. Everything is removed when the app is uninstalled.

Crash reports and usage statistics (Google Firebase)

The app uses Google Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase. If the app crashes, Crashlytics sends a crash report with technical details (for example the phone model, Android version and where in the app the error happened). Analytics sends information about how the app is used, such as screens opened and app version, with device identifiers including an app-instance ID and, where available, the advertising ID. We use this only to fix problems and improve the app, never for advertising. Google processes this data under Firebase's privacy and security terms.

Phone permissions

  • Phone, call log and contacts: needed to act as your default phone app.
  • Notifications and full-screen alerts: to show incoming calls, including on the lock screen.
  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth): to route call audio to a Bluetooth headset.

The app does not use your location, camera or microphone outside of phone calls, and does not record calls on the phone.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising. We share it only with service providers that work for us: the call-center service that connects and records calls, and Google Firebase for crash reports and statistics. We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

Security and retention

Information sent by the app travels over encrypted HTTPS connections. Release versions of the app do not write network traffic to the phone's logs. We keep account information while you work with Adapa, and call records and recordings for as long as needed for the purposes above or as the law requires.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask to see, correct or erase your personal data, withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, and raise a grievance. Contact us using the details below. You can also stop the app collecting anything by signing out and uninstalling it.
